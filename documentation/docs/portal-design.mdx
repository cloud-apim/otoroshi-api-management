# The design of a portal

What a [developer portal](./developer-portal.mdx) looks like and says is its **design**. A portal
has one of three **modes**:

| Mode | What it is | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Default template** | the template of the product, as it is: the title and tagline of the portal, its accent color and logo, a home in Markdown, its pages | a portal up in minutes, with the look of the product |
| **Themed** | the same pages, themed: colors for the light and the dark themes, a font, a radius, a favicon, links in the header and the footer, a style sheet of yours — and a home made of **blocks** | the visual identity of an organisation, without writing a page |
| **Templates** | the home, the overview of an API and the free pages written as **templates** ([Markdoc](https://markdoc.dev), the format of the documentation of Stripe), the components of the portal as tags, on top of the theme | pages of your own, the catalogue, the reference, the plans and the subscription placed where you want them |

In every mode, the pages where a consumer **acts** — subscribing, its applications and their
credentials, its usage, signing in and up — stay the product's, themed: their behaviour, their
security and their translations are not the design's to change.

![A themed portal](/img/screens/portal-themed-home.png)

The design is edited in the console — **Developer portal**, in the sidebar, below Applications —,
from a **folder** with the [CLI](./gitops/cli.mdx), or from a [remote catalog](./gitops/remote-catalogs.mdx).
Nothing of it reaches the gateway: it is the product's, like the documentation of an API.

## A draft, published by versions

The design is a **draft**. **Publish** freezes it into an immutable, numbered **version**, and the
portal shows the **last version published**.

- **Before any version**, the portal shows what its settings describe: a portal that was never
  designed is the default template, unchanged.
- **Preview the draft** opens the portal on the draft, in a new tab, for an hour, with a banner
  *Preview of the draft*. The preview also shows the **errors of the templates** (an unknown tag,
  an attribute of the wrong type), which the published portal never shows; the preview link is the
  only way to see a draft, signed, and it lasts an hour.
- **Versions** lists them (searched by number, comment, mode, author). **Restore** brings a version
  back into the draft; publishing it is the **rollback**.
- Every save of the draft, every publication, restoration, upload of an asset and permission of
  scripts is in the **audit trail**.

![The overview of the page of a portal](/img/screens/console-portal-overview.png)

## The page of the portal, in the console

| Section | What it holds | Saved |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | the url of the portal, the mode of the draft, the version published (when, by whom), the changes of the draft since, **Publish** and **Preview the draft** | — |
| **Settings** | the interface languages and the default one, self signup, local accounts, the [identity provider](./authentication.mdx#portal) of the portal, the discovery signals of the catalogue (reviews, usage), its categories | at once: they are settings, not design |
| **Design** | the mode, the title and the tagline per language, the theme | the draft |
| **Home** | the Markdown of the home (default template), its blocks (themed), or its template (templates) | the draft |
| **Pages** | the free pages: a slug, a title and a content per language, shown in the header or not | the draft |
| **Templates** | the template of the overview of an API (templates mode) | the draft |
| **Navigation** | the links of the header and of the footer | the draft |
| **Styles & scripts** | the style sheet, the script, and whether scripts are **allowed** on the portal | the draft — the permission at once |
| **Assets** | the images, fonts and style sheets of the design: uploaded, referenced, removed | the draft |
| **Versions** | the versions, read only, restored into the draft | — |
| **Sync** | how to work from a folder or a catalog; `portal.yaml` to download | — |

Only the **administrators** of the workspace edit the design, publish it and allow scripts; its
**producers** read the page. A design that a catalog or a folder owns (see below) is read only in the
console, with a banner saying where it is edited.

![The design: the mode, the texts, the theme](/img/screens/console-portal-design.png)

## The theme

| | Mode | |
|---|---|---|
| **Accent** | every mode | the color of the buttons, the links, what is selected |
| **Logo** | every mode | in the header: an url (`https://…`), or an asset of the design (`assets/logo.png`) |
| **Favicon** | themed, templates | the icon of the browser tab: an url or an asset |
| **Font** | themed, templates | a css `font-family` (`Georgia, serif`); a font of your own is an asset, declared with `@font-face` in the style sheet |
| **Corners** | themed, templates | square, small, medium (the product's), large |
| **Colors** | themed, templates | background, surface, text, muted, border — for the **light** theme and for the **dark** one; a color left empty is the product's |
| **Navigation** | every mode | the links of the header and of the footer |
| **Style sheet** | themed, templates | css applied after the product's |

A **link** of the navigation, of a button or of Markdown is one of: an url of the web
(`https://status.example.com`), a mail (`mailto:api@example.com`), a path of the portal (`/apis`), an
anchor (`#plans`), or a page of the design (`page:guide`). Anything else — `javascript:`, `data:` —
is refused.

## The home of a themed portal: blocks

| Block | Fields | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `hero` | a title, a tagline (per language; the texts of the portal by default) | the title, the tagline and a search of the catalogue |
| `featured` | a title, a limit (1–24) | the APIs the catalogue features, the most used otherwise |
| `catalogue` | a title, a category, a tag, a limit | the cards of the APIs of the catalogue |
| `categories` | a title | the categories, each a link to its APIs |
| `markdown` | a content per language | a section of text; its images and links may name an asset or a page |

The blocks are shown in their order; 30 at most.

## Templates

In the mode **Templates**, the **home** (`home`) and the **overview of an API** (`api`) are
templates, and so are the **free pages**. A template is Markdoc — Markdown, with **tags** and
**variables** —, written per language. Nothing else runs in a template: Markdoc renders no html, no
script; its links and images go through the checks of the navigation.

![The home written as a template, the tags beside it](/img/screens/console-portal-home.png)

### The tags

| Tag | |
|---|---|
| `{% catalogue category="retail" tag="…" limit=6 /%}` | the cards of the APIs of the catalogue — of a category, of a tag |
| `{% featured limit=3 /%}` | the featured APIs |
| `{% categories /%}` | the categories, each a link to its APIs |
| `{% api-card api="books" /%}` | the card of one API, by its slug |
| `{% api-reference api="books" /%}` | its reference: its OpenAPI rendered, tried from the page with the credentials of the consumer |
| `{% plans api="books" /%}` | its plans, what each gives, and how to subscribe |
| `{% subscribe api="books" plan="plan_free" label="Get a key" /%}` | a button that subscribes — to a plan, when named |
| `{% button href="page:guide" kind="primary" %}…{% /button %}` | a link drawn as a button (`primary` or `secondary`) |
| `{% callout type="info" title="…" %}…{% /callout %}` | a highlighted note: `info`, `warning`, `success`, `danger` |
| `{% columns %}{% column %}…{% /column %}{% /columns %}` | columns side by side |

A tag that does not exist, or an attribute of the wrong type, is an error: the preview lists them
with their line, the published portal leaves the tag out.

### The variables

| Variable | |
|---|---|
| `{% $portal.title %}`, `{% $portal.tagline %}` | the texts of the portal, in the language of the page |
| `{% $lang %}` | the language of the page |
| `{% $user.signedIn %}`, `{% $user.name %}` | the consumer signed in |
| `{% $api.name %}`, `{% $api.version %}`, `{% $api.description %}`, `{% $api.slug %}`, `{% $api.baseUrl %}` | in the template of an API |

A variable is not read in a code span (`` `{% $api.baseUrl %}` `` is shown as it is): write it as
text.

### An example

The home of a portal written as a template:

```markdown
# {% $portal.title %}

{% $portal.tagline %}

{% button href="page:guide" %}Start with the guide{% /button %}
{% subscribe api="forecasts" plan="plan_free" label="Get a free key" /%}

{% callout type="info" title="New" %}
Forecasts by the minute arrive next month.
{% /callout %}

## Our APIs

{% catalogue limit=3 /%}
```

And the overview of an API:

```markdown
# {% $api.name %}

{% $api.description %} Called at {% $api.baseUrl %}.

{% columns %}
{% column %}
### Hourly
The next 48 hours, hour by hour.
{% /column %}
{% column %}
### Every city
Five thousand cities, and growing.
{% /column %}
{% /columns %}

{% api-reference api="forecasts" /%}
```

## Assets

| | |
|---|---|
| Kinds | images (png, jpg, gif, webp, avif, ico, svg), fonts (woff2, woff, ttf, otf), style sheets (css) |
| Size | 2 MB an asset, 20 MB for a portal |
| Referenced | by its path, `assets/<name>`: the logo, the favicon, an image in Markdown or a template, an `url()` of the style sheet |
| Served | by the portal, by their digest, cached for good, never sniffed; an svg is a document of its own, sandboxed |

A **script is never an asset**: the script of a design is its own field, and it is served only where
scripts are allowed. An asset that the logo or the favicon uses cannot be removed.

## Scripts

A design can hold a **script**, and **it runs only if the administrators of the workspace allow
scripts on the portal** — off by default. Allowing them is an act of its own: the console shows the
warning, and asks to acknowledge it.

:::danger
A script of the design runs in the portal, in the session of every consumer who opens it: it can
read what they see — their applications, their credentials — and act on their behalf. Allow it only
for a script you wrote or reviewed, served by this portal alone.
:::

![Allowing scripts: the warning](/img/screens/console-portal-scripts.png)

- Allowing and forbidding scripts are in the audit trail, with who and when; the page of the portal
  says who allowed them.
- Scripts forbidden, the script of the design is **kept but not served**: forbidding is immediate, and
  takes nothing out of the design.
- Allowing scripts over the admin API: `PUT …/portal/scripts {"enabled": true, "acknowledge": true}` —
  without `acknowledge`, it is refused, the warning in the answer.

### The content security policy of the portal

The page of the portal carries a content security policy, in every mode:

| | |
|---|---|
| Scripts | the portal's own, and its inline ones by a nonce made for each page — **and, where scripts are allowed, scripts of the web** (an analytics tool, a chat) |
| Connections | the portal only — and the web, where scripts are allowed |
| Styles, images, fonts | the portal's, inline styles, and those of the web (a logo or a font at an url) |
| Frames | the portal only; no site frames the portal |
| Others | no plugin, no other base, forms posted to the portal only |

## A folder, a catalog

The design is also a **folder**, the same for the CLI and the catalogs:

```text
portal/
  portal.yaml                 # kind: Portal — the mode, the texts, the theme, the navigation, the blocks
  home.en.md                  # the home of the default template, per language
  home/block-3.en.md          # the content of a markdown block
  pages/guide.en.md           # a page, per language
  templates/home.en.md        # the templates
  templates/api.en.md
  styles.css
  script.js
  assets/logo.png
```

`portal.yaml` names the files it uses, relative to it and within the folder:

```yaml
apiVersion: otoroshi-apim.io/v1
kind: Portal
metadata:
  name: showcase                      # the workspace
spec:
  mode: templates
  texts:
    title: { en: Weather Co developers, fr: Développeurs Weather Co }
    tagline: { en: Forecasts as a service. }
  theme:
    accent: '#0e7c66'
    logo: assets/logo.png
    favicon: assets/logo.png
    font: "Georgia, serif"
    radius: large
    colors:
      light: { background: '#f3f7f5' }
      dark: { background: '#0b1310' }
  navigation:
    header:
      - { label: { en: Guide }, href: 'page:guide' }
      - { label: { en: Status }, href: 'https://status.example.com' }
    footer:
      - { label: { en: Contact }, href: 'mailto:api@example.com' }
  home:
    blocks:
      - { kind: hero }
      - { kind: catalogue, limit: 3 }
  pages:
    - slug: guide
      title: { en: Guide }
      nav: false
      content: { en: { file: pages/guide.en.md } }
  templates:
    home: { en: { file: templates/home.en.md } }
    api: { en: { file: templates/api.en.md } }
  css: { file: styles.css }
  assets:
    - { path: assets/logo.png, file: assets/logo.png }
```

```bash
bun cli/apim.js portal pull showcase portal              # the draft into ./portal
bun cli/apim.js portal push showcase portal --watch      # every change into the draft; the preview link printed
bun cli/apim.js portal push showcase portal --publish    # the draft, then a version
bun cli/apim.js portal preview showcase                  # a link to the draft, for an hour
bun cli/apim.js portal versions showcase
```

`push` sends only the assets the portal does not hold yet; the others are named by their digest.

A [remote catalog](./gitops/remote-catalogs.mdx) — git, S3, HTTP — that holds the same folder applies
the design at each sync and **publishes** it (unless the catalog writes drafts only), and then **owns**
it: the console shows it read only, and a document without a source — a `push` — is refused, unless
it takes the design over (`apim portal push … --force`, `POST …/portal/apply?force=true`). A design
can be owned by one source at a time.

![A folder synchronised with the cli, or a catalog](/img/screens/console-portal-sync.png)

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| A design | 2 MB, its assets aside |
| A text (title, tagline, label) | 500 characters |
| The home in Markdown, a markdown block | 64 KB per language |
| A template, a page | 256 KB per language |
| The style sheet | 200 KB |
| The script | 500 KB |
| Links | 20 in the header, 20 in the footer |
| Blocks | 30 |
| Pages | 30 |

## Questions

- **I published, the portal has not changed.** A browser keeps the previous page: reload it. The
  portal shows the last version published, not the draft: **Preview the draft** shows the draft.
- **A tag does not show.** Open the preview: it lists the errors of the templates.
- **My script does not run.** Scripts are off by default: **Styles & scripts › Allow scripts on this
  portal**. Then publish the design that holds the script.
- **The console refuses to save the design.** It is owned by a catalog or a folder (the banner says
  which): edit it there, or take it over with `--force`.
- **I want my own sign-in page.** The pages where a consumer acts stay the product's, themed; their
  texts follow the languages of the portal.
