# Messages

A person of a portal writes to the workspace from **Messages**: a conversation, asynchronous, an inbox
on each side. Its **subject** — an API, one of the person's subscriptions, or nothing in particular —
routes it to the team that answers:

| Subject | Answered by |
|---|---|
| an API | the team of the API |
| a subscription of the person | the team of its API |
| nothing in particular | the administrators of the workspace |

The administrators of the workspace see every conversation; a producer, the ones routed to their
teams. The text of a message is Markdown.

## In the portal

**Messages**, in the header — the count beside it says the conversations the workspace answered since
they were read —: the conversations of the person, searched and paged, open or closed. **New
conversation** asks for its subject, a title and a first message. An API page offers **Ask a
question**, and each subscription of *My apps* **Ask about it**: the subject already chosen. In a
conversation, the person's own messages are on the right; **Close the conversation** when it is
settled — writing again reopens it.

## In the console

**Messages**, in the sidebar — the count beside it says the conversations waiting for an answer —:
the conversations one answers, open first, searched by title, subject or person. A conversation
opens on a page of its own: its messages in order, the ones of the workspace on the right, an answer
written below; **Close** or **Reopen** it.

## Told

Each side is told of the other's message in its notifications — `message.received` to the team that
answers (the administrators of the workspace when there is none), `message.reply` to the person —,
and by [email](./concepts/applications-and-subscriptions.mdx#by-email) when the workspace sends those
kinds so. Each person reads a conversation on their own: one producer reading it does not read it
for another.

The conversations are in the audit trail — opened, a message, closed, reopened, never their text —,
and so reach the [webhooks](./webhooks.mdx) of the workspace (`conversation.*`).

```bash
otoroshi-apim messages list default --status open
otoroshi-apim messages reply default conv_01a2… --text "Yes: the Gold plan takes 20k calls a day."
```
